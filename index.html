Stretch cursorTry itHow it works

Move the mouse. Quickly.

A ring that trails the pointer, stretches in the direction of travel, and fills in over anything clickable. It inverts against whatever is underneath, so it stays visible on any color.

You are on a touch device, so you get the normal cursor. That is on purpose. Try this on a computer.

Things to hover

Links, buttons and form fields all trigger the filled state.

How it behaves

Mouse users only. Touch screens keep the native cursor. If someone has reduced motion turned on, the ring follows the pointer without the trailing or the stretch.
